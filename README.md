# PSU Calculator

## Accurate Power Supply Estimation for Modern Gaming PCs

Choosing the right power supply is one of the most important steps when building or upgrading a computer. A **PSU Calculator** helps users estimate the total power requirements of their PC and recommends the ideal power supply wattage based on hardware configuration.

Whether you are creating a budget desktop, a gaming machine, or a professional workstation, a PSU Calculator makes the process easier by calculating estimated energy consumption from all major components.

This tool is useful for gamers, PC enthusiasts, streamers, developers, video editors, and anyone planning a custom computer build.


# What is a PSU Calculator

A PSU Calculator is an online tool that estimates how much electrical power a computer system requires. The calculator analyzes hardware components and generates a recommended PSU wattage range for stable operation.

The tool usually considers:

- Processor power usage
- Graphics card requirements
- Cooling systems
- Storage devices
- RAM modules
- Motherboard consumption
- Peripheral devices

The final result helps users select a reliable power supply unit for their system.


# Why PSU Selection is Important

Many users focus heavily on CPUs and GPUs but ignore the importance of the power supply. A low quality or underpowered PSU can create serious system issues.

## Problems Caused by Incorrect PSU Selection

- Unexpected shutdowns
- Blue screen errors
- Random reboots
- Performance instability
- Reduced component lifespan
- Overheating risks
- Upgrade limitations

Using a PSU Calculator helps avoid these problems before purchasing hardware.


# How a PSU Calculator Estimates Power Usage

The calculator combines the estimated wattage of all selected components.

## Step 1: CPU Selection

The processor is one of the primary power consuming components in any PC.

Examples:
- Intel Core processors
- AMD Ryzen processors

Higher core counts and overclocking increase power requirements.

## Step 2: GPU Selection

Graphics cards usually consume the highest amount of power in gaming systems.

Examples:
- NVIDIA RTX series
- AMD Radeon series

Modern GPUs may require:
- Dedicated PCIe connectors
- Higher wattage headroom
- Stable voltage delivery


## Step 3: Storage Devices

Storage devices also contribute to total power usage.

Supported devices include:
- SATA SSD
- NVMe SSD
- HDD
- External drives

Multiple drives increase power consumption slightly.


## Step 4: Cooling and RGB

Additional cooling hardware affects PSU requirements.

Examples:
- RGB fans
- Liquid coolers
- Pump systems
- Fan controllers

Large gaming setups with extensive RGB lighting require additional wattage.

# Understanding PSU Efficiency Ratings

Efficiency ratings indicate how effectively a PSU converts electricity.

## Common PSU Certifications

- 80 Plus Bronze
- 80 Plus Silver
- 80 Plus Gold
- 80 Plus Platinum
- 80 Plus Titanium

Higher efficiency PSUs generally offer:
- Lower temperatures
- Reduced power waste
- Quieter fan operation
- Improved reliability

# Modular Power Supplies

## Fully Modular PSU

All cables can be detached for:
- Cleaner cable management
- Better airflow
- Easier maintenance

## Semi Modular PSU

Some cables are fixed while others are removable.

## Non Modular PSU

All cables are permanently attached.

These are usually more affordable but create additional cable clutter.
